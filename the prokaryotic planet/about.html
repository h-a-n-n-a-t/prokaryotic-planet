ABOUT THE PROJECT

Making the bacterial world navigable.

The Prokaryotic Planet is designed as an interactive scientific knowledge interface: less rummaging through an ocean of information, more guided exploration.

🧭

Explore

Move naturally from taxonomy to genus, species, biology, disease, resistance, therapeutics and research.

📚

Organize

Research records are separated into general, disease and therapeutic pathways so users can reach the information they need quickly.

🔌

Connect

The frontend is API-ready for future scientific sources including NCBI Taxonomy, PubMed, BacDive, UniProt, KEGG, BV-BRC, GTDB, CARD and ResFinder.

SCIENTIFIC DATA NOTE

Prototype ≠ complete bacterial census.

The included records are demonstration data. A production version should retrieve, normalize, cite and update real records from authoritative databases and should never claim complete coverage unless that coverage is actually established.
